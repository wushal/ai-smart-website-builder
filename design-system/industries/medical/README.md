# Medical Industry Design System


## Industry Positioning


Medical websites should communicate:


- Trust
- Professionalism
- Safety
- Scientific authority
- Human care


The website should make users feel:

"This is a reliable medical organization."


---

# Visual Direction


## Keywords


Use:


- Clean
- Calm
- Professional
- Clinical
- Human-centered


Avoid:


- Futuristic AI style
- Cyber style
- Neon colors
- Excessive animations
- Gaming visual language


---

# Color System


Primary:


Healthcare Blue


Purpose:

Trust and reliability.



Secondary:


Soft Green


Purpose:

Health and recovery.



Background:


Warm White


Purpose:

Clean medical environment.


Accent:


Light Cyan


---

# Typography


Recommended:


Headings:

Clear and authoritative.


Body:

Readable and comfortable.


Avoid:


- Extremely large hero text
- Aggressive typography


---

# Motion Rules


Use:


- Fade in
- Gentle slide up
- Scroll reveal


Duration:


300-600ms


Avoid:


- Particle effects
- Heavy parallax
- Floating 3D objects


---

# Content Principles


Medical content should focus on:


- Clinical value
- Patient benefits
- Professional expertise
- Safety information


Avoid:


Generic marketing phrases.


Example:


Bad:

"Revolutionizing healthcare with AI"


Good:

"Helping doctors improve diagnosis efficiency through intelligent clinical assistance."
