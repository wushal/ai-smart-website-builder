# Ecommerce Industry Design System


## Industry Positioning


Ecommerce websites should communicate:


- Desire
- Trust
- Convenience
- Conversion


Goal:


Drive purchasing decisions.


---

# Visual Direction


Use:


- Product-focused
- Clean
- Attractive
- Conversion-oriented



Avoid:


- Excessive technical style
- Complex animations



---

# Color System


Depends on brand.


Recommended:


- White background
- Brand primary color
- Product highlight color



---

# Content Principles


Focus:


- Product benefits
- Reviews
- Discounts
- Trust signals
- Purchase motivation
