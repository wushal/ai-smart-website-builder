# Ecommerce Components Rules


Preferred:


## Product Showcase


Include:


- Product image
- Price
- Benefits
- CTA



---


## Product Comparison


Help users decide.



---


## Reviews


Include:


- Customer comments
- Ratings
- Photos



---


## Trust Section


Include:


- Shipping
- Payment security
- Guarantee



---


Avoid:


- SaaS pricing cards

- Complex technical diagrams
